Riso printing

We take on small risograph print jobs. A risograph prints one ink at a time through a stencil, a lot like screenprinting. Colors come out flat with some grain, and the layers don't always line up exactly.

Request a quote

Sending files

  1. 1One file per inkSplit your artwork into one layer per ink, and save each layer in black and gray.
  2. 2Name each file by inkFor example, poster_blue.pdf and poster_yellow.pdf.
  3. 3Leave a marginA risograph can't print to the edge of the paper. Keep artwork at least 1/4 in from every edge.
  4. 4Send the filesPDFs at actual size, plus a color mockup of how it should look.
