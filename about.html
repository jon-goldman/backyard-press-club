About

Two people, one risograph

Backyard Press Club is Ezgi Eyigor and Jon Goldman. We make risograph prints from our own 35mm photographs and from old family pictures, and print them in small editions, signed and numbered in pencil. Our first run sold at the Brooklyn Fine Art Print Fair.

The studio

We print on RISO MZ790 and ME9450 machines, one ink per pass, mostly on 11 × 17 Speckletone. Every piece starts as a set of separations we make ourselves, one plate for each ink, and gets proofed on plain paper before the real run.

We also print small runs for other people. See Riso printing.
