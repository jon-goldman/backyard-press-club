About

Backyard Press Club is Ezgi Eyigor and Jon Goldman. We make risograph prints from our own 35mm photos and from old family photos. Most are small editions, signed and numbered in pencil. We first sold them at the Brooklyn Fine Art Print Fair.

The studio

We print mostly on 11 × 17 Speckletone. We make the separations ourselves, one for each ink, and print each ink in its own pass. We proof on plain paper before printing the edition.

We also print for other people. See Riso printing.

Who we are

Ezgi Eyigor shoots 35mm film. Green Water is from her photo of the water at Akyaka, on the Turkish coast.

Jon Goldman makes the separations and scans old family photos. Matador is from one of them.
