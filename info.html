Care

Riso ink never dries completely, so it can smudge if you rub it. Hold prints by the edges and don't stack them face to face.

Keep them out of direct sun. The inks fade in strong light, the bright ones first. Glass over the print helps, and UV glass helps more.

Questions

What is a risograph?

A stencil printer from Japan that looks like a photocopier. It prints one ink at a time, so a four-color print goes through the machine four times. There is more on the Riso printing page.

Will my print look exactly like the picture?

Close, but not exactly. The pictures here are scans of one copy. Each copy comes out a little different: the inks line up slightly differently and the color can be heavier or lighter. Screens also show the colors differently from paper.

Are the prints signed?

Editioned prints are signed and numbered in pencil. Postcards are an open run and aren't signed. Each print's page says which it is.

Can I pick up an unframed print in Brooklyn?

Yes. Message us before you order and we'll sort it out so you don't pay for shipping.
